| Requirement | Status | Evidence |

The line is absent while the calendar query has no payload (`:227`, `:288`). |

**Independent verification that the two zone-extreme cases discriminate (the closure condition).** The two required cases are `DashboardPage.test.tsx:331-347`:

- **Case 1 (UTC+14, `Pacific/Kiritimati`)** — expects `Today, Oct 30, 2026 · Pacific/Kiritimati` and **no** `Oct 31, 2026`.
- **Case 2 (UTC−12, `Etc/GMT+12`)** — expects `Today, Oct 30, 2026 · Etc/GMT+12` and **no** `Oct 29, 2026`.

Reverting the construction to the superseded **noon-UTC anchor re-rendered in the profile zone** (`` formatDate(new Date(`${today}T12:00:00Z`), timeZone) ``) gives `2026-10-30T12:00Z` → at UTC+14 that is `2026-10-31T02:00` → **"Oct 31, 2026"**, so **case 1 fails** (both its assertions: the expected text is absent and the forbidden text is present). At UTC−12 the same anchor is `2026-10-30T00:00` → "Oct 30, 2026", so **case 2 still passes** under that particular revert. Reverting instead to a **midnight-UTC anchor re-rendered in the profile zone** gives `2026-10-30T00:00Z` → at UTC−12 that is `2026-10-29T12:00` → **"Oct 29, 2026"**, so **case 2 fails**. The pair therefore rules out **both** single-anchor constructions — exactly the contract the plan states ("no single UTC-anchored instant renders the payload's civil date correctly in every zone"). The implementer's fix round additionally recorded executing both cases against each reverted construction and observing the corresponding failure (`artifacts/EP05B/implementation.md` §4.1, row F-V6); **this review did not re-execute them** (no shell) — the behaviour above is derived from the code, which is sufficient to establish the discrimination.

**Conclusion: F-EP05B-01 / PC-EP05-13 is closed.** The corrected construction is in the shipped code, the two required boundary cases are present and discriminating, and the client still performs no profile-zone arithmetic.
